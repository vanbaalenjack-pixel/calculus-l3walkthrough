2025 NCEA Level 2 Calculus Question 2(b)

2025 Paper — Find the original function from \(f'(x)\)

← Back to paper

Question

\[ f'(x)=2x^3-6x^2+2x+4 \]

The graph of \(f(x)\) passes through \((2,5)\).

Find the equation of \(f\).

Antidifferentiate first, then use the point \((2,5)\) to work out the constant.

First walkthrough idea

Tip to try first

Antidifferentiate each term in \(f'(x)\) separately and remember to add \(+c\).

Step 1

Antidifferentiate term by term

Reveal the explanation and working for this step.

Show the first step’s working

Antidifferentiate \(f'(x)=2x^3-6x^2+2x+4\) term by term.

Worked result

\[ \frac{1}{2} x^{4} - 2 x^{3} + x^{2} + 4 x + c \]

Integrating term by term gives \(\frac{1}{2}x^4-2x^3+x^2+4x+c\).
