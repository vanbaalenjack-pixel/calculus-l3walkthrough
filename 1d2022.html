2022 NCEA Level 3 Differentiation Question 1(d)

2022 Paper - Parametric differentiation

Question

A curve is defined parametrically by the equations:

\[ x=2+3t \qquad \text{and} \qquad y=3t-\ln(3t-1), \qquad t>\frac{1}{3} \]

Find the coordinates, \((x,y)\), of any point(s) on the curve where the tangent to the curve has a gradient of \(\frac{1}{2}\).

You must use calculus and show any derivatives that you need.

A later walkthrough visual becomes available with JavaScript; the complete question and first learning steps remain below.

First walkthrough idea

Tip to try first

For parametric curves, the gradient is found from \(\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{dy/dt}{dx/dt}\).

Step 1

Find \(\frac{dx}{dt}\)

Reveal the explanation and working for this step.

Show the first step’s working

Differentiate \(x=2+3t\) with respect to \(t\).

Worked result

\[ 3 \]

The derivative of \(2+3t\) with respect to \(t\) is just \(3\).
