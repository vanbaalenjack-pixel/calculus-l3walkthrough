Question

Solve the following equation for \(m\), where \(m\) is a real number.

\[ |5-mi|=\sqrt{5m^2} \]

First walkthrough idea

Focus to try first

rewriting a modulus as a square root, squaring the equation, and solving for a real parameter.

Step 1

Rewrite the modulus

\(|5-mi|\) becomes \(\sqrt{5^2+m^2}\).

Show the first step’s working
\[ |a+bi|=\sqrt{a^2+b^2} \]

\(|5-mi|\) becomes \(\sqrt{5^2+m^2}\).

Key result

\(\,\sqrt{5^2+m^2}=\sqrt{5m^2}\)
