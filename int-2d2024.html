Question

The graph below shows part of the graph of the function \(y=\sin^2x\).

Find the shaded area enclosed between the lines \(y=\sin^2x\), \(y=1\), \(x=-\frac{\pi}{2}\), and \(x=\frac{\pi}{2}\).

You must use calculus and show the results of any integration needed to solve the problem.
