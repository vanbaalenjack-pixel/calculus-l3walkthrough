2020 NCEA Level 3 Integration Question 3(d)

2020 Paper

← Back to paper

Question

In radioactive decay, the rate at which a radioactive substance decays at any instant is proportional to the number of radioactive atoms present at that instant. This can be modelled by \(\frac{dN}{dt}=kN\), where \(N\) is the number of radioactive atoms present and \(t\) is the time in days.

A quantity of manganese-52 is produced. Manganese-52 is a radioactive isotope of manganese and has a half-life of \(5.6\) days; after \(5.6\) days, half of any atoms of manganese-52 would have decayed.

How long would it take for \(95\%\) of the manganese-52 to decay?

You must use calculus and give the results of any integration needed to solve this problem.

First walkthrough idea

Focus to try first

Solve the separable decay equation, use the half-life to find \(k\), then set the remaining proportion to \(0.05\).

Step 1

Solve the differential equation

Separate \(N\) and \(t\), then integrate.

Show the first step’s working
\[ \frac{1}{N}\,dN=k\,dt \] \[ \ln|N|=kt+C \] \[ N=N_0e^{kt} \]
