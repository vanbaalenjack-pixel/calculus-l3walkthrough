Question

The complex numbers \(u\) and \(w\) are represented on the Argand diagram below.

\[ \text{If } z = 2u + 3w,\text{ find } z,\text{ and clearly show it on the Argand diagram above.} \]

First walkthrough idea

Focus to try first

reading complex numbers from an Argand diagram, scaling them, and adding them to find a new point.

Step 1

Read \(u\)

The point \(u\) is at \((4,2)\), so \(u=4+2i\).

Show the first step’s working

The point \(u\) is at \((4,2)\), so \(u=4+2i\).

Key result

\(\,u=4+2i\)
