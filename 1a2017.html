Question

\[ \text{Differentiate }y=\sqrt{x}+\tan(2x). \]

First walkthrough idea

Focus to try first

Treat the square root as a power, then differentiate the tangent term as a composite function.

Step 1

Rewrite the square root

Writing a radical as an exponent makes the power rule visible.

Show the first step’s working
\[\sqrt{x}=x^{1/2}\]

For \(x^n\), the power rule is

\[\frac{d}{dx}(x^n)=nx^{n-1}.\]

Common mistake to avoid

Do not stop after differentiating the outside function; include the derivative of the inside function as a factor.
