Question

Julia's friend Sarah believes that the equation of the curved border of the paved courtyard can be modelled by the function

\[ y=\frac{15x-15}{x+2}. \]

Use integration to find the area of the courtyard, shown in the diagram above.

You must use calculus and show the results of any integration needed to solve the problem.

The diagram is shown in its initial state. JavaScript adds any interactive controls and later walkthrough visuals.

First walkthrough idea

Focus to try first

Rewrite the rational function as a constant minus a reciprocal linear term, then integrate from \(x=1\) to \(x=11\).

Step 1

Rewrite the numerator

Create a multiple of \(x+2\), leaving a constant remainder.

Show the first step’s working
\[ 15x-15=15(x+2)-45, \] \[ y=\frac{15(x+2)-45}{x+2} =15-\frac{45}{x+2}. \]
