Question

The graph below shows the curves \(y=3\sec^2x\) and \(y=2\tan^2x\).

Find the area of the shaded region enclosed by the two curves, \(x=1\), and the \(y\)-axis.

You must use calculus and show the results of any integration needed to solve the problem.

The diagram is shown in its initial state. JavaScript adds any interactive controls and later walkthrough visuals.

First walkthrough idea

Hint to try first

Between \(x=0\) and \(x=1\), the top curve is \(y=3\sec^2x\) and the bottom curve is \(y=2\tan^2x\).

Step 1

Identify top minus bottom

Area between curves is top minus bottom over the interval \(0\le x\le1\).

Show the first step’s working

Area between curves is top minus bottom over the interval \(0\le x\le1\).
