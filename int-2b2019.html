Question

The graph of \(y=f(x)\) has the \(y\)-axis as a line of symmetry. It crosses the \(x\)-axis at \(-A,-B,B,A\). Each shaded region below the axis, from \(-A\) to \(-B\) and from \(B\) to \(A\), has area \(1.2\).

If \(\int_{-A}^{A}f(x)\,dx=5.8\), what is the value of \(\int_{-B}^{B}f(x)\,dx\)?

First walkthrough idea

Focus to try first

Use signed area: the full integral includes two negative shaded regions, while \(\int_{-B}^{B}f(x)\,dx\) excludes them.

Step 1

Identify the signed pieces

The two shaded side regions are below the \(x\)-axis, so each contributes \(-1.2\) to the signed integral.

Show the first step’s working
\[ \int_{-A}^{-B}f(x)\,dx=-1.2 \] \[ \int_B^A f(x)\,dx=-1.2 \]
