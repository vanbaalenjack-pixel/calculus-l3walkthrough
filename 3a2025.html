Question

The graph below shows the function \(y=f(x)\).

\[ \text{(i) Find the value(s) of }x\text{ where }f(x)\text{ is not differentiable.} \] \[ \text{(ii) Find the value(s) of }x\text{ where }f'(x)=0. \] \[ \text{(iii) What is the value of }\lim_{x\to -2}f(x)\text{?} \]

The diagram is shown in its initial state. JavaScript adds any interactive controls and later walkthrough visuals.

First walkthrough idea

Hint to try first

Points are not differentiable where the graph has a jump, a hole, or a sharp break.

Step 1

Find the non-differentiable points

There is a break at \(x=-6\), a hole and mismatched point at \(x=-2\), and another hole at \(x=3\).

Show the first step’s working

There is a break at \(x=-6\), a hole and mismatched point at \(x=-2\), and another hole at \(x=3\).

Key result

\[ x=-6,\,-2,\text{ and }3 \]
