2025 Paper — Rebuild the original function from its gradient

← Back to paper

Question

\[ f'(x)=2x^2+2x-24 \]

Find the equation of the original function \(f(x)\) if the \(y\)-value of the local minimum is \(-46\).

Use the derivative to find the critical points, use the second derivative to identify the minimum, then integrate and solve for the constant.

First walkthrough idea

Tip to try first

Solve \(f'(x)=0\) first to find the critical points.

Step 1

Find the critical points

Reveal the explanation and working for this step.

Show the first step’s working

Set the derivative equal to zero and solve for both critical x-values.

Worked result

\[ -4,\ 3 \]

\(2x^2+2x-24=0\) simplifies to \(x^2+x-12=0\), which factors to \((x+4)(x-3)=0\).
