Question

\[ \text{Write }\frac{12k}{1+\sqrt{5}}\text{ in the form }ak+bk\sqrt{5},\text{ where }a\text{ and }b\text{ are integers.} \]

First walkthrough idea

Focus to try first

multiplying by the conjugate, using the difference of squares, and finishing in the exact form \(ak+bk\sqrt{5}\).

Step 1

Identify the conjugate

The conjugate of \(1+\sqrt{5}\) is \(1-\sqrt{5}\).

Show the first step’s working

The conjugate of \(1+\sqrt{5}\) is \(1-\sqrt{5}\).

Key result

\(\,\frac{1-\sqrt{5}}{1-\sqrt{5}}\)
