2025 Calculus questions

Start paper

Question 1

Q1(a) Using the power rule term by term and evaluating the gradient at a point. Q1(b) Finding the point of tangency, the gradient, and the tangent equation. Q1(c) Finding critical points, identifying the minimum, and solving for the constant. Q1(d) Writing displacement equations, solving when they are equal, and answering in context.

Question 2

Q2(a) Sketching the derivative from stationary points, signs, and changes in concavity. Q2(b) Antidifferentiating and using a point on the graph to find the constant. Q2(c) Differentiating, evaluating a rate, and using derivatives to justify the minimum turning point. Q2(d) Using a stationary point and an x-intercept to find constants, then classifying the other stationary point.

Question 3

Q3(a) Differentiating, solving for the given gradient, and finding the coordinate on the curve. Q3(b) Using the base–height relationship to find dA/dh, with area in cm² and height in cm. Q3(c) Using a surface-area constraint, writing volume in one variable, maximizing it, and proving the maximum. Q3(d) Differentiating, factorising, classifying critical points, and finding where the function is decreasing.
