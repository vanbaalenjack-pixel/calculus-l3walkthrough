Question

\[ 2\log_5(x+6)-\log_5 x-2=0 \]

Solve the equation.

First walkthrough idea

Hint to try first

Move the \(2\) to the other side, then combine the logarithms on the left.

Step 1

Combine into one logarithm

\(2\log_5(x+6)=\log_5((x+6)^2)\), so the argument becomes \(\frac{(x+6)^2}{x}\).

Show the first step’s working
\[ 2\log_5(x+6)-\log_5 x=2 \] \[ \log_5(\square)=2 \]

\(2\log_5(x+6)=\log_5((x+6)^2)\), so the argument becomes \(\frac{(x+6)^2}{x}\).

Key result

\[ \frac{(x+6)^2}{x} \]
