Question

Find \(\int\sec(2x)\tan(2x)\,dx\).

First walkthrough idea

Focus to try first

Recognise the reverse chain-rule derivative of \(\sec(2x)\).

Step 1

Use the sec-tan derivative

Because \(\frac{d}{dx}\sec(2x)=2\sec(2x)\tan(2x)\), compensate with a factor of \(\frac12\).

Show the first step’s working
\[ \int\sec(2x)\tan(2x)\,dx =\frac12\sec(2x)+C \]

Final answer

\[ \boxed{\frac12\sec(2x)+C} \]
