Skill overview

What these questions practise

Choose a question, attempt it independently, then compare the reasoning and restrictions in its walkthrough.

Relevant standards: NCEA Level 3 Complex Numbers — AS91577

This collection contains 31 matching walkthroughs across 9 examination years.

The grouping is based on the method recorded for each walkthrough. Use the linked NZQA standard and paper material for authoritative wording, diagrams, and assessment information.

Before you start

Common mistakes in these methods

  • A modulus is a nonnegative distance. Translate the original distance statement carefully and check any sign restriction introduced by squaring.
