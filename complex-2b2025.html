Question

\[ u = m\operatorname{cis}\left(\frac{3\pi}{10}\right) \]

Write \(u^5\) in the form \(a+bi\), where \(a\) and \(b\) are both real numbers, giving your answer in terms of \(m\).

First walkthrough idea

Focus to try first

using De Moivre’s Theorem and then converting the result into \(a+bi\) form.

Step 1

Identify the theorem

De Moivre tells us how the modulus and argument change when we raise a complex number to a power.

Key result

Common mistake to avoid

Apply the power to the modulus and multiply the argument by the same power before converting form.
